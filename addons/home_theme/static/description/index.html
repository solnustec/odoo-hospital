A real app launcher

Launch, find and arrange — like your phone

The home screen turns every installed app into a tile you tap to open. Find any screen in a keystroke, and shape the grid to the way you work.

Tap to launch

A responsive grid of every installed app. One click opens the app — no digging through the top menu.

Instant search

Start typing — or press Ctrl + K — to find any menu and jump to it. Accent-insensitive: “facturacion” finds “Facturación”.

Dark mode, included

A complete dark recompilation of the backend — not just the navbar. Light / Dark / System per user, with a fully customizable dark palette.

Drag to reorder

Arrange apps exactly how you like, or group them by category. Your layout is saved per user.

Your own wallpaper

Pick one of thirteen bundled wallpapers or upload your own; the theme adapts icon contrast so everything stays readable.

Keyboard first

Arrow keys move across the grid, Enter opens the app, Esc clears — and typing anywhere lands in the search box.

Icons your size

Small, medium or large tiles in one click — the grid re-flows and your choice is remembered.

Instant search — type and jump to any menu or screen.
Dark mode & dark palette — free

The Enterprise dark mode, in Community

Flip the whole backend to a tuned dark theme — each user picks Light, Dark or System from the user menu — and fine-tune its palette: brand, primary, semantic colours and navbar. At no cost.

Included free: the full backend recompiled dark.

Theme colours

Make it match your brand

A simple Settings page with a live preview. Tune the brand, primary, info, success, warning and danger colours, the navbar and the home app names — for both light and dark mode, side by side — then reset anytime.

Light and dark palettes, side by side, with live preview.

A bundled gallery right in Settings: six backgrounds colour-matched to the theme palettes, three signature designs and four light, business-friendly looks. Pick one and it becomes your home screen — no upload needed.
