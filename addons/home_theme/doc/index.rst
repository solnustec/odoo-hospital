Home Screen Theme
=================

A modern, customizable home screen for Odoo 17 Community - a clean grid of all
your apps with theme colours, a personal wallpaper and drag-and-drop ordering.

Installation
------------

1. Copy the ``home_theme`` folder into your custom addons path.
2. Restart the Odoo server.
3. Open the **Apps** menu and click **Update Apps List**.
4. Search for **Home Screen Theme** and click **Install**.

Configuration
-------------

Open **Settings -> Home Screen Theme** (administrator):

- **Backend colours** - customise the light-mode palette: brand, primary, info,
  success, warning and danger colours; the navbar / sidebar background and text;
  and the home app-name colour. Use **Reset light colors** to restore defaults.
- **Home screen background** - upload your own wallpaper (JPG, PNG or SVG;
  1920×1080 or larger is recommended).

Usage
-----

- The home screen becomes the launcher: a responsive grid of every installed
  app, with a greeting and an instant search bar - type to find any app, menu
  or screen and jump straight to it.
- Drag an app tile to reorder it; the layout is saved per user.
- Switch to the *group by category* view to organise apps into sections.
- Move between the home screen and your apps with the back / home arrows.

A full backend dark mode, a vertical sidebar, a global command palette and more
are available in the **Home Screen Theme Pro** add-on.
